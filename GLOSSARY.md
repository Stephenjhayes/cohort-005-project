# Cadence

Cadence is a course platform where learners buy courses, work through their lessons, and can ask for their money back.

## Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask to get their money back on a purchase, with their reason. It is Open until our team decides, then Approved or Declined. A purchase can have several Refund Requests over time, but only one Open at a time.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback, Pending Chargeback, Requested, Pending (for the request's status), Refund (when you mean the ask)

**Refund**:
The money returned to a learner, created when a Refund Request is Approved. It is Pending until the money is sent, then Issued or Failed. A Failed Refund leaves its Refund Request Approved and is retried with a new Refund. A purchase has at most one Issued Refund, always for the full Refundable Amount.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Refundable Amount**:
How much a Refund for a purchase returns: exactly what the learner paid for it, whatever the course costs now.
_Avoid_: Refund amount, reversal amount, list price

**Refund Window**:
The days after a purchase during which a learner may raise a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cancellation window

**Refund Eligibility**:
The single set of rules that decides whether a learner may raise a Refund Request on a purchase: inside the Refund Window, under the watch-time and lesson-completion limits, and not a claimed team seat. Every place that offers or accepts a Refund Request applies the same rules.
_Avoid_: Cancellation offer, can cancel

**Refund rate**:
Issued Refunds divided by purchases. Pending and Failed Refunds, and Declined Refund Requests, do not count.
_Avoid_: Chargeback rate, cancellation rate

## Out of scope

- **Chargebacks** (a cardholder disputing a charge with their bank) are not part of Cadence's domain. Never use "chargeback" for a Refund Request or a Refund.

## Open questions

- Can staff raise a Refund Request on a learner's behalf, for example a goodwill refund outside the Refund Window? For now, only the learner raises one.
- Can a learner withdraw an Open Refund Request? There is no such state today.
- How do Refund Requests work for team licences, such as unclaimed seats?
